# Delete your Random Thoughts account

## In the app (instant)

1. Open **Random Thoughts**.
2. Go to **Me** → **Delete account**.
3. Enter your password and confirm.

This permanently deletes:

- your account and email address,
- your display name, friend code, and friend list,
- every thought and photo you shared, **including your friends' copies**,
- thoughts your friends sent you, and your pending requests.

## Without the app

Email `YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL` from the address you signed up with, with the
subject **"Delete my Random Thoughts account"**. We'll delete everything
listed above within 30 days and confirm by email.
