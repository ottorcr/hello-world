# Random Thoughts: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

Random Thoughts is a small, free app for sharing short thoughts and photos
with friends you approve. It has no ads, no tracking, and no payments.

**Contact:** `YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL` ← replace before publishing

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Email address and password (stored hashed by Google Firebase Authentication) | To sign you in |
| Display name and friend code | So friends can find and recognize you |
| Your friend list, friend requests, and blocks | To deliver thoughts only to approved friends |
| Thoughts and photos you share | To show them to your friends |
| Reports you file | To review abusive content |

Photos are resized on your phone, and their metadata (including GPS
location) is removed **before** upload.

We do **not** collect your contacts, location, device advertising ID, or
analytics, and we don't sell or share your data.

## Who can see it

- Your thoughts and photos: only the friends you had when you shared them.
- Your email: nobody except you (and the app operator, for support).
- Friend codes: anyone who has your code can send you a request. Nothing is
  shared until you approve it.

## Where it's stored

The app uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) as a data
processor. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. The widget keeps a
copy of your friends' recent thoughts on your own device, and it is removed
when you sign out.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Unsending a thought removes it from every friend. Deleting
your account removes your account, friendships, and **everything you
shared**, including your friends' copies. Reports may be kept for up to 90
days to handle abuse.

## Your rights

You can delete your account in the app (*Me → Delete account*) or by
following [these steps](delete-account.md). If you're in the EU/EEA or UK,
you can also ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or restriction of
processing by emailing the contact above. You also have the right to
complain to your local data protection authority.

## Age

You must be 18 or older to use Random Thoughts.

## Changes

We'll update this page and the date above if anything changes.
